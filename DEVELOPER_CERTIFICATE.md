### Platform Overview: AgriBone

**AgriBone** (Agriculture + Backbone) is an innovative AgriTech platform that empowers farmers, agronomists, and agricultural businesses through digital transformation:

- **Farm Management** - Complete farm operations tracking
- **Marketplace** - Agricultural products and equipment trading
- **Analytics Dashboard** - Data-driven farming insights
- **Community Platform** - Farmer networking and knowledge sharing
- **Inventory Management** - Seeds, livestock, and equipment tracking
- **Financial Tools** - Farm accounting and budgeting
- **Weather Integration** - Climate data for farming decisions
- **PWA Support** - Installable mobile-first application
